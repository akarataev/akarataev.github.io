Tone Profiler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Tone Profiler is designed to help guitar players discover songs and related signal chain settings. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app or contact us.

Information We Collect

The app may store local preferences on your device, such as favorite songs and recent search history. This information is used to personalize the app experience and is not used to identify you.

If you submit a form, such as a song request or contact message, we may receive the information you choose to provide, including your email address and message content.

How We Use Information

  • To provide app features such as favorites and search history.
  • To respond to messages and song requests.
  • To improve the app, catalog, and support experience.

Third-Party Services

Forms on this site may be processed by Formspree. When you submit a form, your submission is handled according to Formspree's own privacy practices.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app. Form submissions may be retained as long as needed to respond to your request and maintain support records.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us through the contact form in the app.
