Tone Profiler

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

Tone Profiler is designed to help guitar players discover songs, tones, and related signal chain information. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app or interact with related Tone Profiler web pages.

Summary

The Tone Profiler app does not require an account. Favorites and recent search history are stored locally on your device. We receive personal information only if you choose to submit it through a contact, song request, or issue report form.

Information We Collect

Local app data. The app may store information on your device, such as favorite songs and recent search history. This information is used to provide app features and personalize your experience. It is not sent to us by the app.

Form submissions. If you submit a contact form, song request, or issue report, we may receive the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, song title, artist name, message, notes, and issue details.

Technical web data. When you open or submit a web form, our website hosting providers and form provider may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser or device information, date and time of request, and form delivery metadata.

How We Use Information

  • To provide app features such as favorites and search history.
  • To respond to messages and song requests.
  • To review and correct reported catalog issues.
  • To improve the app, catalog, and support experience.
  • To maintain the safety, reliability, and integrity of our app and related web pages.

How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use the app to track you across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

We may share or process information with service providers that help us operate Tone Profiler, such as form processing, website hosting, email delivery, and app distribution providers. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services to us.

Third-Party Services

Forms on this site are processed by Formspree. When you submit a form, your submission is handled according to Formspree's own terms and privacy practices. Tone Profiler may also rely on Apple services for app distribution and related App Store functionality.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you remove it through available app controls or uninstall the app. Form submissions may be retained as long as needed to respond to your request, maintain support records, improve the catalog, and protect the app from misuse.

Your Choices

  • You can choose not to submit forms or provide optional information.
  • You can remove local app data by deleting relevant app content where controls are available or by uninstalling the app.
  • You can contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information you submitted through a form.

Children's Privacy

Tone Profiler is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

International Processing

Your information may be processed in the country where you live or in other countries where we or our service providers operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from the laws where you live.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Tone Profiler evolves. The latest version will be available from the app profile screen or related website pages.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want to make a privacy request, please contact us through the contact form in the app.
